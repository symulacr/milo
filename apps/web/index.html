Plain answers

What does “private” mean?
The ledger hides the price, brief and files. Not anonymous, and a proof does not judge the work.
Is approval payment?
No. Capture is separate and can fail.
Wrong delivery?
Do not approve. Open a dispute: the operator has 48 hours and cannot change the terms.
Lost your key?
It lives in this browser; Milo cannot replace it. Keep the encrypted backup safe.
What do I need?
Chromium and the Lace wallet on Preprod, with tDUST for fees. Get tNIGHT, then “Generate tDUST”.
