Privacy and boundaries

The planned protocol protects confidential terms from public observers. Milo, authorized participants and the chosen proving path have different access. A proof is not a quality guarantee.

Understand who sees what ↗

For creative studios

Product goals for the pilot — stated as goals until pilot evidence exists.

Fewer ambiguous approvals

The buyer reviews the exact submitted files and approves against the agreed scope, with a record of what was approved.

Clearly bounded work

One fixed price, one fixed delivery of three final images, agreed deadlines and a named resolution path. No open-ended revision rounds.

An exportable record

The confirmed order record can be inspected and exported, so your files and the approval history stay with you.

Payment is handled by a separate provider and is never guaranteed by approval. Disputes go to the pre-agreed operator; they are not eliminated.

Questions, answered plainly

What software do I need?

A tested desktop browser and a designated wallet profile, chosen when you prepare an order — never at landing. Compatibility evidence for that profile is still being gathered. Reading a quote needs no wallet; on an unsupported phone you can read everything and resume on a tested desktop after signing in again.

Who pays execution costs?

On the current test profile, each participant covers their own execution capacity; the actual requirement is shown before any action that needs it. Sponsored execution is a deferred experiment with no selected sponsor — there is no “free forever” promise.

What does “private” mean?

Commercial terms stay off the public ledger. Milo, the merchant, the payment provider and the chosen proving path each see the data they actually receive. Private does not mean anonymous, and a proof is not a quality guarantee.

What if I lose my approval capability?

A recovery kit preserves your approval capability and is verified before any payment hold. Without it, an order can become read-only — signing in again cannot recreate it.

How long are the deadlines?

Each order carries agreed delivery and review deadlines, shown with timezone before you consent. The current 24-hour delivery and 12-hour review windows are initial product hypotheses, not validated market requirements.

Which devices are supported?

Tested desktop browsers first. Other devices can read quotes and orders; consequential actions stay on the tested profile.

Can I cancel an order?

The buyer can cancel before the merchant accepts. After acceptance, the pre-agreed operator resolves disputes in full — approval or cancellation, never a partial rewrite of the agreement.
